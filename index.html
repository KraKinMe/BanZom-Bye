Today is my last day. Instead of a plain goodbye email, I shipped this — because git blame should sometimes be git thanks. As a DevOps engineer, I rarely wrote the features you shipped, but I got to keep the lights on while you did — the pipelines green, the deploys boring, and the rollbacks (mostly) unnecessary. Thank you for trusting me with your prod and forgiving me for the occasional 3 a.m. alert. 🚀
